AnswerGuard — Request data deletion

App: AnswerGuard: Spam Call Shield  ·  Developer: Igor Ganapolsky (iganapolsky@gmail.com)

This page explains how to delete the data AnswerGuard handles. AnswerGuard is a privacy-first call screening app: nearly everything stays on your device. The instructions below cover the two classes of data — on-device, and the small amount we collect through analytics and crash diagnostics.

Option 1 — Delete on-device data from inside the app

This is the fastest path and removes the data immediately.

From the AnswerGuard home screen:
  1. Open AnswerGuard.
  2. Tap Settings (top-right gear).
  3. Tap Delete my data.
  4. Confirm. AnswerGuard will clear your screening history, personal blocklist, and contacts allowlist immediately.

You can do this any time without uninstalling the app. Your AnswerGuard settings remain so you can keep using call screening.

Option 2 — Uninstall the app

Uninstalling AnswerGuard removes all on-device data automatically — screening history, blocklist, allowlist, and any cached state. Android handles this as part of the standard uninstall flow.

Option 3 — Request deletion of analytics and crash data

If you also want us to delete the limited analytics and crash data we have collected from your device, email iganapolsky@gmail.com with the subject “AnswerGuard data deletion request”.

What to include in your email so we can find your records:

  • The Google account email used to install AnswerGuard (if applicable), or
  • The AnswerGuard installation ID shown in Settings → About (a random UUID like a1b2c3d4-...).

We will delete the analytics events and crash reports associated with that identifier within 30 days and confirm by email. There is no charge for this request.

What gets deleted

  • On-device data (deleted by Option 1 or 2): screening history, personal blocklist, contacts allowlist cache, app preferences, the per-install random analytics identifier.
  • Off-device data (deleted by Option 3): the anonymous PostHog analytics events tied to your installation ID (e.g. “app opened”, “call screening enabled”, “paywall viewed”); Firebase Crashlytics crash reports from your device.

What we keep

  • Subscription records (if you bought AnswerGuard Pro). Google Play retains the purchase record under Google's own retention policy — we cannot delete that on Google's behalf. To manage or cancel a subscription, use the Google Play Store app or visit play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
  • Aggregated, non-identifying metrics (e.g. “total active installs this week”) that contain no individual identifier. These cannot be tied back to you.

Retention

On-device data persists until you delete it via Option 1 or 2. Off-device analytics and crash data that you have not requested deletion for is retained for up to 13 months under our service providers' standard retention policies (PostHog, Firebase).
