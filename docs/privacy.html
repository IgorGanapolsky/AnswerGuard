AnswerGuard Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 21, 2026

AnswerGuard (“the app”, “we”) is a call-screening app for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

The short version

AnswerGuard screens calls entirely on your device. Your contacts, the phone numbers of calls you receive, your screening history, and your personal blocklist never leave your phone. We do not sell your data, and the app contains no ads.

Information processed only on your device

  • Contacts. With your permission, AnswerGuard checks an incoming number against your contacts so people you know are never silenced or blocked. This check happens entirely on your device. Your contacts are never uploaded or shared.
  • Incoming call numbers. When a call arrives, AnswerGuard evaluates the number on your device to decide whether to allow, silence, or block it.
  • Screening history and blocklist. The list of recently screened calls and the numbers you choose to block are stored only on your device.

None of the above is transmitted off your device.

Information we collect

To improve the app, AnswerGuard collects limited information through these services:

  • Product analytics (PostHog). Anonymous usage events — for example, when the app is opened, when call screening is enabled, and when a purchase screen is viewed — associated with a random identifier generated by the app. This identifier is not your name, phone number, or device advertising ID. Analytics events never include the phone numbers of your calls or contacts.
  • Crash diagnostics (Google Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes, a crash report (including device model and Android version) is collected to help us fix it.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing). If you buy AnswerGuard Pro, Google processes the payment. The app records whether you have an active subscription.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • The app contains no advertising and does not collect an advertising identifier.
  • We do not upload your call history, contacts, or messages.

Data retention and your choices

On-device data (screening history, blocklist) remains until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all on-device data. Analytics and crash data are retained by our service providers under their standard retention periods.

Children

AnswerGuard is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date above.
