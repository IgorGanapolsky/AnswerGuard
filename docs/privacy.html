AnswerGuard Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 27, 2026

AnswerGuard (“the app”, “we”) is a call-screening app for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

The short version

AnswerGuard screens calls entirely on your device. Your contacts, the phone numbers of calls you receive, the text of incoming SMS messages, your screening history, and your personal blocklist never leave your phone. We do not sell your data, and the app contains no ads.

Information processed only on your device

  • Contacts. With your permission, AnswerGuard checks an incoming number against your contacts so people you know are never silenced or blocked. This check happens entirely on your device. Your contacts are never uploaded or shared.
  • Incoming call numbers. When a call arrives, AnswerGuard evaluates the number on your device to decide whether to allow, silence, or block it.
  • Incoming SMS messages (Pro tier). With your permission, AnswerGuard reads the sender and body of incoming SMS solely to identify the caller (e.g. delivery notifications, two-factor codes, business sender identity). Message content is processed on-device and is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared. We do not read, store, or analyse SMS from people in your contacts.
  • System call log and visual voicemail. With your permission, AnswerGuard reads the Android system call log and voicemail entries to surface calls that bypassed its real-time screening (for example, calls silenced by Do Not Disturb or routed direct-to-voicemail by your carrier’s spam filter). These entries are merged into the in-app Recent Activity view on your device only.
  • Screening history and blocklist. The list of recently screened calls and the numbers you choose to block are stored only on your device.

None of the above is transmitted off your device.

Information we collect

To improve the app, AnswerGuard collects limited information through these services:

  • Product analytics (PostHog). Anonymous usage events — for example, when the app is opened, when call screening is enabled, and when a purchase screen is viewed — associated with a random identifier generated by the app. This identifier is not your name, phone number, or device advertising ID. Analytics events never include the phone numbers of your calls or contacts, and never include SMS content.
  • Crash diagnostics (Google Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes, a crash report (including device model and Android version) is collected to help us fix it.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing). If you buy AnswerGuard Pro, Family, or Business, Google processes the payment. The app records which tier (if any) is currently active so it can unlock the corresponding features. We do not see your payment instrument or billing address — that information stays with Google.

Subscription tiers

AnswerGuard offers three optional paid tiers via Google Play Billing:

  • AnswerGuard Pro — SMS caller identification and advanced spam rules.
  • AnswerGuard Family — everything in Pro plus the on-device AI intent analysis, voice biometrics, and shared protection for up to six household members. Family sharing is implemented via Google Play’s standard family-sharing infrastructure; we do not collect or store the email addresses or identities of family members ourselves.
  • AnswerGuard Business — everything in Family plus business-tuned spam rules and priority support. Entitlement is granted per Play account; we do not collect business names, employee identities, or organisation data.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • The app contains no advertising and does not collect an advertising identifier.
  • We do not upload your call history, contacts, voicemail, or SMS messages.
  • We do not transmit the body of any SMS message to any server.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete all on-device data at any time:

  • From inside the app: Settings → “Delete my data”. This clears your screening history, blocklist, contacts allowlist, screening pause state, and the one-time prompts the app has shown you. (This option ships in AnswerGuard v1.2.8.)
  • From Android Settings: Apps → AnswerGuard → Storage → Clear data. This has the same effect as the in-app delete and additionally clears the Play Billing cache.
  • By uninstalling the app: Removes all on-device data immediately.

Analytics and crash data sent to PostHog and Firebase Crashlytics are tied to a random per-install identifier and are retained by those service providers under their standard retention windows. To request deletion of analytics or crash data tied to your installation, email iganapolsky@gmail.com with the request and we will forward it to the relevant processor within 30 days.
