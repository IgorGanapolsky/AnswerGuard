AnswerGuard helps reduce spam and scam call interruptions with privacy-first call protection.

AnswerGuard is designed around conservative spam decisions, transparent controls, and local-first protection. The goal is simple: block confirmed spam, reduce suspicious interruptions, and avoid hiding legitimate calls.

Why AnswerGuard:

- Privacy-first spam protection
- No account required
- Clear setup and status checks
- Conservative blocking criteria
- Built for fewer missed real calls

AnswerGuard does not sell your data or use call protection data for advertising.
