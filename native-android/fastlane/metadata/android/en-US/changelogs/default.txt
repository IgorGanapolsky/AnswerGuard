What's new in v1.2.7:
- Recent Activity now shows weekday, date, and time for screened calls so older entries are not just a time of day.
- Recent Activity now explains why carrier-routed voicemail and Do Not Disturb calls may not appear when Android routes them before AnswerGuard can screen them.
- New launcher icon: shield + telephone handset, so the app is recognisable on your home screen at a glance.
- "Delete my data" in the Privacy & Data card — clear your screening history and blocklist without uninstalling.
- Refreshed privacy policy with explicit sections for SMS, Family/Business plans, and on-device data deletion.
