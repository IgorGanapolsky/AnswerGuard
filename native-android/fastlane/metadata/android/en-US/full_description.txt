AnswerGuard is a privacy-first call screening app for Android. It silences spam, blocks robocallers, and shows you exactly why each call was handled the way it was — all on your device, with no account required.

What's in the box today:

- ON-DEVICE SCREENING: every incoming number is evaluated locally with heuristics tuned for 2026's spam landscape. Decisions in well under a second; no cloud round-trip, no audio leaves your phone.
- TRANSPARENT ACTIVITY LOG: see whether each recent call was Allowed, Silenced, or Blocked, plus calls your carrier routed straight to voicemail or that Do Not Disturb silenced before AnswerGuard's screening hook could fire.
- PERSONAL BLOCKLIST: tap to block any number from Recent Activity, or manage your full blocklist in one place.
- CONTACTS ALLOWLIST: people saved in your contacts are never silenced. Contact lookup happens on-device only.
- DELETE MY DATA: clear your screening history, blocklist, and allowlist any time, without uninstalling.
- ZERO ADS, NO TRACKERS: no advertising ID, no data-broker connections, no account.

Plans:

- Basic (free): local heuristic screening, personal blocklist, contacts allowlist, transparent activity log.
- AnswerGuard Pro: advanced spam rules and priority routing for high-risk numbers.
- AnswerGuard Family: Pro features for up to six family members via Google Play family sharing.
- AnswerGuard Business: Pro plus business-tuned spam rules and priority support.

Roadmap (not yet in this release):

- On-device AI intent analysis powered by Gemini Nano.
- Voice biometric defense against AI voice-cloning scams.

AnswerGuard is built for users who want strong protection without giving up their privacy. Source code, privacy policy, and feature roadmap at https://igorganapolsky.github.io/AnswerGuard/
